READ THE FOOTNOTE

The Boston Housing dataset is historical and has serious ethical limitations, including a problematic racial variable represented in the source feature B. TerraVista keeps the original inputs for reproducibility, but these estimates should not guide real-world lending, pricing, or decisions about people and communities.
